# Phase 2-C k Phase 3 Cost Report

Status: proxy_only_no_change

Required k values evaluated: 64, 128, 192, 256, 320, 512. PJP1 and the Phase 3 preflight remain byte-tensor consumers of the incumbent k=256 layout. Larger k values scale polar sections linearly and would require a contract change, retraining of readers, and real-label evidence. No k change is promoted.
